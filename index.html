WhyStory

One surprising "why" from history and science, whispered in about a minute.

WhyStory is an educational short-video channel on YouTube (@WhyStoryDaily). Each episode answers a single question, such as why 1900 wasn't a leap year or why flamingos are pink, with facts checked against published sources that are listed in every video's description.

About the WhyStory uploader

WhyStory uses YouTube API Services. The WhyStory uploader is a private tool, run only by the channel's owner, that uploads the channel's own finished videos to the WhyStory YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to other users and does not access any other channel's data.
